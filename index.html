About This Project

Note: All descriptive and interpretive text throughout this interactive — including component labels, descriptions, and contextual callouts — is placeholder content developed for prototype purposes. It has not been reviewed against primary sources or verified by a curator, and should not be treated as authoritative. All content is subject to revision prior to any public publication.

This interactive is a proof of concept developed by the Wolfsonian Digital Labs as part of an ongoing exploration of object-based digital interpretation.

Content note: the covers include sexualized and violent imagery, shown here for critical discussion.

  1. Choose a cover. Four paperbacks from four genres: a literary reprint, a crime novel, an adventure story and an adult paperback.
  2. Take a first look. Give the cover a few seconds before reading anything. Notice where your eye goes first.
  3. Look closely. Tap the glowing points to see how each part of the cover works. Choose a lens, such as the gaze, gender or race, to show only those details.
  4. Read the book and its times. The Book and Context tabs cover who made it, what it was about and how it was sold.
  5. Analyze. The Analysis tab reads the whole cover through each lens.
  6. Respond. Answer a question in your own words. Your responses collect under My responses, and Start over clears them for the next visitor.
